Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This page explains the data handling associated with Oqerun Office Suite, based on the published application code and its intended operation. Hosting providers and third-party services may process technical information under their own policies.

Data handled by the application

  • Documents: Writer content and drafts are stored in browser localStorage and the application’s browser-based virtual file system by default.
  • Imports and exports: supported document, spreadsheet, and presentation files are processed in the browser. The app does not need to upload document content to a VRuntime Labs server for its basic local editing workflow.
  • External resources: if you follow a link or open a third-party resource from a document, that service may receive standard request data. Avoid adding sensitive content to remote links.
  • Browser security: browser-local storage is not encrypted by this application and may be readable by scripts executing in the same origin or by anyone with access to your browser profile.
  • Hosting: GitHub Pages may process standard access logs and technical request data.

Storage and backups

Clearing browser site data can permanently remove documents and drafts. Export important work regularly and keep backups outside the browser. Do not treat local browser storage as secure cloud storage or a backup service.

Service providers and external links

Oqerun Office Suite is hosted through GitHub Pages. Office documents may contain links to external resources, which are handled by your browser if you choose to open them.. Links to third-party sites, repositories, application publishers, and services are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not control those services.

Retention and deletion

Where this application stores information in your browser, you can generally remove it by clearing the relevant site data. This may permanently remove local documents, preferences, or installed-app records. Server-side files, if any, follow the retention described above. Do not submit passwords, session cookies, access tokens, or other secrets unless a feature explicitly requires them and you understand the risks.

Children

This project is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information was submitted inappropriately, contact the maintainer using the project’s GitHub repository. Do not post personal or sensitive details in a public issue.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the application’s data practices change. For questions or privacy requests, use the contact method listed on the VRuntime Labs GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive information in a public issue. A dedicated privacy contact should be published before broad public launch.

This policy is informational and is not a legal opinion. Applicable requirements depend on the operator, audience, jurisdictions, and actual deployment configuration. Have it reviewed for the jurisdictions you serve.
